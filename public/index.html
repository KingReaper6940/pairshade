Palette contrast matrix

Measure the actual colors.

Pairshade uses the WCAG 2.x sRGB relative luminance formula. AA needs 4.5:1 for normal text or 3:1 for large text (at least 24 CSS px, or about 18.67 px bold).

A check, not a certification.

Ratios cover opaque, solid colors only. Transparency, gradients, images, rendering and other accessibility requirements need separate review. The sample preview deliberately shows your selected colors, including failing pairs.
